# Wantace Solutions Engineer Intern Assignment

## Overview

This repository contains my submission for the **Wantace Solutions Engineer Intern Assignment**. The assignment focuses on understanding real business operations, identifying workflow challenges, documenting requirements, and communicating practical solutions.

My approach is to understand the business problem before recommending technology, keeping the proposed solution grounded in observations and stakeholder needs.

## Assignment Sections

### Part A — Business Audit

Conducted an audit of a local tailoring business to understand its day-to-day operations, order tracking process, and delivery challenges.

**Key observation:** A shortage of skilled workers for blouse handwork can affect order completion and delivery timelines.

**Learning:** Not every operational problem can be solved through software. Understanding the actual cause is essential before recommending a solution.

### Part B — Requirements Document

Analysed the fictional case study of Vardhan Steelware to identify its key operational challenges and define the requirements for a practical first-phase solution.

**Key problem:** Limited visibility into order status and delays across the workflow.

**Proposed Phase 1 focus:**
- Centralised order register
- Order status tracking
- Partial dispatch and return tracking
- Polishing and quantity reconciliation
- Payment-hold visibility
- Daily operational reports
- Data validation and an audit trail

Advanced AI forecasting and marketplace integrations are outside the proposed initial scope.

### Part C — Account Executive Handoff

Prepared a handoff note covering the stakeholders, business pain points, available evidence, potential objections, and recommended next steps.

The main objective is to help the sales and solutions teams align around the client's actual needs before committing to a solution or timeline.

### Part D — Marketing Content

Created marketing content based on the operational insights from the business audit, while keeping the audited business and fictional client anonymous.

Deliverables:
- LinkedIn post highlighting operational challenges and the importance of order visibility
- Meta ad concept using Hinglish to communicate the benefits of simple order tracking

### Part E — Reflection and AI Use

Reflected on the key learnings from the business visit, the underlying problem in the Vardhan Steelware case, and one decision that could be improved through additional validation.

The AI-use note explains how AI supported drafting and organisation, where its suggestions needed critical review, and why evidence-based decisions remain important.

## Key Learnings

- Understand the real workflow before proposing technology.
- Separate the client's requested features from the underlying business need.
- Validate inconsistent records rather than making assumptions.
- Consider the needs and working habits of every stakeholder.
- Keep the first phase focused on solving the most important problem.
- Use AI as an assistant, not as a replacement for observation, verification, or judgement.
