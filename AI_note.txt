# AI Use Note

1. I used ChatGPT to help organise my assignment and structure the requirements document, handoff note, and marketing drafts.
2. I used it to improve clarity, organise the business problems, and present the findings in a professional format.
3. One limitation I noticed was that AI can suggest generic technology solutions before fully considering the actual business workflow.
4. For example, AI dashboards and forecasting may sound useful, but Vardhan’s immediate need is reliable order-status visibility.
5. I kept the Phase 1 scope focused on order tracking, status updates, partial dispatches, and operational reports.
6. I treated inconsistent order records as items requiring verification rather than assuming that every discrepancy had a known explanation.
7. I also reviewed the drafts to ensure that they did not introduce unsupported business facts or promises.
8. The business observations and final decisions should reflect my own visit, review, and judgement.
